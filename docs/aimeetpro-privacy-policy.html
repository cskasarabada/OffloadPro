AIMeetPro

Privacy Policy

Your recordings and transcripts stay on your device. AIMeetPro only sends audio data to third-party AI services when you explicitly choose to transcribe or summarize, using API keys that you provide and control.

What AIMeetPro Does

AIMeetPro is a voice recording app with AI-powered transcription and summarization. It records audio on your device, provides live transcription during recording, and can generate enhanced transcripts and summaries using AI services.

Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored locally on your iPhone:

  • Audio recordings (M4A files)
  • Transcripts (generated via on-device Apple Speech or AI services)
  • AI-generated summaries
  • Your settings and preferences

This data remains on your device unless you choose to export it. You can delete any recording and its associated data at any time from within the app.

API Keys You Provide

AIMeetPro uses third-party AI services for transcription and summarization. To use these features, you enter your own API keys in the app's settings. Your API keys are stored locally on your device and are never shared with us or any other party. The supported services are:

  • Groq — for Whisper-based audio transcription
  • OpenAI — for Whisper transcription (alternative provider)
  • Anthropic (Claude) — for AI-powered summaries

When you transcribe or summarize a recording, audio data or transcript text is sent directly from your device to the AI provider using your API key. We never see, store, or relay this data. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle the data they receive.

On-Device Transcription

AIMeetPro also offers on-device transcription using Apple's built-in Speech Recognition framework. When you use this mode, your audio is processed entirely on your device and no data is sent over the internet.

Microphone Access

AIMeetPro requests access to your device's microphone to record audio. Microphone access is only used during active recording sessions that you initiate. Audio is never captured in the background.

Analytics & Crash Reporting

AIMeetPro may collect anonymous usage analytics and crash reports to help us improve app stability and user experience. This data does not include any personally identifiable information, recording content, transcripts, or API keys. You can opt out of analytics sharing through your device's iOS Settings under Privacy & Security.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your audio recordings or their contents
  • Your transcripts or summaries
  • Your API keys
  • Your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • Your location data

No Advertising or Tracking

AIMeetPro does not display advertisements and does not use any advertising SDKs or user tracking frameworks. We do not sell or share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Children's Privacy

AIMeetPro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account creation or collect any personal identifiers.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be posted on this page with a revised date below.
